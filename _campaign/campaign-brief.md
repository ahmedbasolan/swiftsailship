# Swift Sail Shipping — "Trade Without Borders" Campaign Brief
**Duration:** 4 weeks · June 2 – June 29, 2026  
**Posting days:** Monday, Wednesday, Sunday  
**Channels:** Personal LinkedIn (Zubair Basolan) + Company page (Swift Sail Shipping LLC)  
**Primary CTA:** Read blog → qualify leads → get quote  
**Tone:** Conversational, expert, grounded in real news  

---

## Campaign Objective

Generate inbound leads from SME importers/exporters, large enterprise supply chains, e-commerce businesses, and UK/Europe companies looking to enter UAE markets — by anchoring content to live trade news rather than evergreen fluff. Each blog post leads with a real event, delivers genuine operational insight, and ends with a soft but clear CTA to get a quote.

**Primary KPI:** Blog sessions from LinkedIn referral traffic  
**Secondary KPIs:** LinkedIn post engagement rate (target 3–5%), quote form fills attributed to content, follower growth on both profiles  
**Reporting cadence:** Weekly review of LinkedIn analytics + website traffic

---

## ⚡ CONTENT MANDATE — Hook First, Always

**Every single post must open with a scroll-stopping hook.** The purpose of all content is to capture attention and pull people toward Swift Sail — not to inform passively. Generic blessings, dry updates, or informational posts with no hook are not acceptable.

**Hook formula:**
- Line 1: Provocative statement, surprising fact, or bold claim that stops the scroll
- Line 2: Expand — why it matters to THIS audience
- Line 3 onward: Deliver the value (insight, tip, story, angle)
- Final lines: Soft CTA — "DM me", "Read more", "We handle this"

**Content that will never be posted:**
- Generic seasonal greetings with no brand angle (e.g. "Wishing you a happy Eid from all of us")
- Informational posts that read like press releases
- Posts that bury the hook after two or more preamble lines
- Any post that could be copy-pasted word-for-word by a competitor

**Every post should make someone stop, think, and either engage or remember Swift Sail.** Be creative. Be direct. Prioritise curiosity and relevance over polish.

## Content Philosophy

Every post hooks on a real news event from the past 2–4 weeks. The blog gives the full analysis. The LinkedIn post teases the one insight the audience won't get anywhere else unless they click through. No recycled industry takes. No "logistics is evolving" openers.

**Monday:** Blog goes live. LinkedIn post (both profiles) drives traffic with news hook + blog link.  
**Wednesday:** LinkedIn-only insight post — one operational tip, data point, or behind-the-scenes angle related to the week's theme. No blog link.  
**Sunday:** LinkedIn engagement post — a question, a hot take, or a short opinion piece to drive comments. Builds algorithm reach for the following Monday launch.

## LinkedIn Post Structure

**Monday launch post (both profiles):**  
Opening line that names the news event → one contrarian or non-obvious angle → two-line tease of the blog's key insight → "Full breakdown on the blog" + URL

**Wednesday insight post:**  
Open with a specific operational detail most people get wrong → explain it clearly in 3–5 short paragraphs → end with a question or "if this has hit you, DM me"

**Sunday engagement post:**  
One sentence that takes a real position or asks a genuine question → 3–4 lines of context → "Drop your answer below" or "Curious what others are seeing"

## Personal vs Company LinkedIn Tone

**Zubair (personal):** First-person. Written from 15 years in UAE freight. "I've seen this with clients." "We had a shipment last week where..." Specific, grounded in real experience. Never corporate.

**Company page:** Third-person or "we". Slightly more formal but still conversational. Leads with the business implication. Ends with a clear quote CTA on Monday posts.

| Risk | Mitigation |

| News hook becomes stale mid-campaign | Each blog is written to have 3–6 month shelf life beyond the news hook. The FTA angle stays relevant through 2026. |
| LinkedIn posts get low reach | Sunday engagement posts rebuild algorithm momentum each week. Reply to every comment personally. |
| Blog traffic doesn't convert | Each blog has a specific CTA tied to the article topic (e.g., "getting your first GCC-UK shipment?" not just "get a quote"). |
