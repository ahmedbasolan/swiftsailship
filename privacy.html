Privacy Policy

How we handle information you share with Swift Sail Shipping through our website.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Swift Sail Shipping L.L.C. ("Swift Sail", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through swiftsailship.com, why we collect it, and how you can contact us.

Information we collect

When you submit a quote request or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name and company name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Shipment details you provide (origin, destination, cargo type, message)
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and submission timestamp (via our hosting provider logs)

How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and prepare freight or customs quotes
  • Communicate with you about services you requested
  • Maintain business records required for UAE trade and logistics operations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

How we store and share data

Quote submissions are processed through our website infrastructure and delivered to our team by email. We may share information with carriers, customs authorities, or service partners only when necessary to fulfil a shipment you have engaged us to handle.

Data retention

We retain enquiry and shipment records for as long as needed to respond to your request, manage ongoing business relationships, and meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements in the UAE.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, subject to legal and contractual obligations. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
