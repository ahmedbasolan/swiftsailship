---

## Step 2 — Write the Hook First (Line 1 is everything)

Line 1 must pass the scroll-stop test. Ask yourself: would someone mid-scroll pause here?

**Hooks that work:**
- Surprising fact: "The GCC-UK FTA was signed 6 days ago. Most of your freight forwarders haven't read it."
- Bold claim: "48-hour customs clearance is real. But only if your documents are right."
- Contrarian angle: "Everyone's celebrating the FTA. Nobody's talking about what gets you denied."

**Hooks that get rejected immediately:**
- "We are pleased to share..."
- "Exciting news from Swift Sail..."
- "Wishing everyone a wonderful..."
- Any line that could start a competitor's post

---

## Step 3 — Write TWO versions (Personal + Company)

### Personal (Zubair Basolan)
- First-person throughout
- Ground it in real experience: "I've seen this with clients", "We had a shipment last week where..."
- Conversational. No bullet-point overload.
- End: "DM me" or "Link in comments 👇"
- Max length: ~250 words

### Company (Swift Sail Shipping LLC)
- "We" or third-person
- Business-first framing. Leads with commercial implication.
- May use ✓ bullets for structured info (clearance times, document types)
- Monday only: ends with direct quote CTA → `📞 +971 55 342 4700 | info@swiftsailship.com`
- Max length: ~200 words

## Quality Bars

| Dimension | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Specific, surprising, or bold | Generic, soft, or preamble-first |
| Voice (personal) | Reads like a human freight expert | Reads like a press release |
| Voice (company) | Professional but conversational | Corporate boilerplate |
| Length | Tight — every sentence earns its place | Padded, repetitive, or meandering |
| CTA | Clear and specific | Vague "learn more" |
